Invoice Generator vs InvoiceSimple

Both claim to be free. Only one actually is. Here's our full comparison.

When searching for a free invoice generator, you'll likely come across InvoiceSimple. It's heavily marketed, has good brand recognition, and promises free invoicing. But is it actually free? We spent time testing both tools — InvoiceGen and InvoiceSimple — to give you an honest breakdown. The short answer: they're not the same. The differences matter if you're a freelancer or small business trying to avoid subscription fees.

Signup Requirements

InvoiceGen requires zero signup — open the page and start creating immediately. InvoiceSimple requires account creation with email verification before you can create your first invoice. For freelancers who want to create a quick invoice without commitment, this friction matters. It also raises data privacy questions — does InvoiceSimple retain your business and client data?

With our free invoice generator, you can create your first invoice in under 60 seconds with no account.

Free Tier Invoice Limits

InvoiceSimple's free tier limits you to 3 invoices total (not per month — ever). After 3, you must upgrade to their paid plan ($9.99-$19.99/month). InvoiceGen has no limits — create 1 invoice or 1,000 invoices, it's always free. For a freelancer billing multiple clients monthly, InvoiceSimple becomes a monthly expense very quickly.

Watermarks on Free PDFs

InvoiceSimple adds visible "Created with InvoiceSimple" branding to free PDFs. This looks unprofessional and signals to clients that you're using a budget tool. InvoiceGen PDFs have zero watermarks — they're completely clean and professional. Your clients see your business name and logo, nothing else.

US Sales Tax Support

Both tools handle basic sales tax. InvoiceGen includes a dedicated sales tax percentage field plus an EIN (Employer Identification Number) field — critical for US businesses working with enterprise clients who need it for 1099 reporting. InvoiceSimple's free tier has partial tax support with no EIN field.

Try our online invoice generator with full US tax field support.

Sharing Options

InvoiceGen offers PDF download, WhatsApp sharing via Web Share API (or direct wa.me link fallback), and browser print. InvoiceSimple allows email sending from their platform on paid plans only — free tier just gives you a PDF download. For freelancers working internationally or with clients who primarily use WhatsApp (common in Australia, UK, South Asia), InvoiceGen's WhatsApp sharing is a genuine advantage.

Speed & Performance

InvoiceGen loads instantly as a static site with no backend calls during invoice creation. InvoiceSimple has a more complex UI with account management, templates library, and recurring invoices that introduces loading latency. For creating a quick one-off invoice, InvoiceGen is measurably faster.

If you need a 100% free invoice tool with no signup, no limits, no watermarks — InvoiceGen is the clear choice. InvoiceSimple is a better fit if you need features like recurring invoices, expense tracking, or client portal — but you'll need to pay for those. For freelancers, contractors, and small businesses who just need clean professional PDF invoices, InvoiceGen delivers everything without the friction or cost.
